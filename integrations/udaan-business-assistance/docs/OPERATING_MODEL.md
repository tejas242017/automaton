# Udaan Operating Model

## Positioning

Udaan is an affordable business-assistance hub for Indian entrepreneurs. The first service lines are:
1. Low-cost documentation and registration coordination.
2. Website and online presence.
3. Meta/social media marketing.
4. Business operations setup (invoicing/POS/catalogue/reporting).
5. Business-plan and funding-readiness preparation.

Public-money funding is a later, separate workstream and must not launch until its legal structure and permissions have been reviewed by qualified Indian professionals.

## People

- **Business-services partner:** customer discovery, requirement clarification, registration-agent coordination, quotations and follow-ups.
- **Udaan founder/technology lead:** website, workflow tooling, automation, customer tracking and digital-marketing delivery.
- **External professionals:** CA/CS/lawyer or other appropriately qualified/authorized professionals for work requiring specialist status.
- **Automaton:** internal drafting and checklist assistant only; it does not become the professional of record.

## Customer workflow

1. **Enquiry:** Record only name/contact preference, service category, city/state and the minimum context needed. Explain what data is needed and why.
2. **Triage:** Mark the request as documentation, website, marketing, operations, or funding-readiness.
3. **Scope:** Human confirms deliverables, exclusions, price, third-party/government charges, expected timeline and refund/cancellation terms.
4. **Consent:** Obtain consent before collecting or sharing documents with a provider. Use an approved storage system—not the agent's prompt/history as a document vault.
5. **Document checklist:** Agent drafts a checklist from approved templates. Staff verifies current official requirements.
6. **Execution:** Authorized staff/provider performs submission or registration. AI can prepare drafts, not submit them.
7. **Quality control:** Staff checks names, numbers, attachments and customer authorization before delivery.
8. **Close-out:** Provide receipt, work summary, outstanding obligations and official links. Never claim approval until verified from the issuing authority.
9. **Follow-up:** Offer optional website, marketing or operations services without making them compulsory for affordable documentation.
10. **Audit:** Log staff member, action, timestamp, approval and evidence location; avoid putting identity documents into logs.

## Simple case status

`new → needs_clarification → quoted → awaiting_consent → documents_pending → in_progress → human_review → completed`

Alternative terminal states: `on_hold`, `cancelled`, `referred_to_professional`.

## Pricing principles

- Quote Udaan's service fee separately from government fees, taxes and CA/CS/lawyer or other provider charges.
- Identify optional services as optional.
- The proposed 1% funding fee applies only if the funding product is legally approved and the agreement precisely defines its fee base and triggering event.
- Do not claim a government charge where none exists. For example, Udyam registration through the official government portal is free; any fee from Udaan must be clearly for optional assistance, not the government registration itself.
- Never guarantee a licence, tax outcome, funding, sales, marketing results or investment return.
