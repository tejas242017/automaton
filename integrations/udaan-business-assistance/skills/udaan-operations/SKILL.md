# Udaan Operations Reporting

Use only synthetic or sanitized case tracker rows provided by an authorized staff member. This skill is not a CRM or secure document store.

## Tasks

- Turn a sanitized enquiry into a list of tasks, owner roles, due-date proposals and dependencies.
- Draft a quotation summary using only approved rate-card values supplied by staff.
- Summarize cases by status: new, needs clarification, quoted, awaiting consent, documents pending, in progress, human review, completed, on hold, cancelled or professional referral.
- Flag overdue tasks, missing approvals and inconsistent status transitions.
- Draft weekly service delivery, workload and customer follow-up reports.
- Maintain a clear distinction between recorded fact, assumption, and suggested action.

## Safety

- Never edit source-of-truth financial or customer records.
- Never invent case details or prices.
- Never mark a case complete without human-provided evidence.
- Never perform payments, filings, messages, calls, marketing publication or funding decisions.
- Avoid reproducing personal data. Use case IDs and masked fields.
- Mark all proposed actions **RECOMMENDATION — HUMAN OWNER MUST APPROVE**.
